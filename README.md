# 🌿 Fruitful — Walk in the Spirit

> *"But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control. Against such there is no law."*
> — Galatians 5:22–23 (NKJV)

---

## About

**Fruitful** is a daily accountability app rooted in the fruits of the Spirit. It is designed not to replace the Holy Spirit, but to help believers stay attentive to His work in their lives — tracking spiritual growth, building community accountability, discerning character in relationships, and leaning into scripture daily.

All scripture references use the **New King James Version (NKJV)**.

---

## Features

- **Daily check-in** — track which fruits of the Spirit you walked in today
- **Weekly focus fruit** — app suggests your weakest area; you can override
- **Journal** — morning intention, evening reflection, gratitude, tomorrow's plan
- **Prayer builder** — AI-generated NKJV-grounded personal prayers
- **Discernment** — prayerfully assess the fruits of the Spirit in people you're discerning
- **Community groups** — create or join accountability groups with invite codes
- **Harvest report** — monthly AI-generated spiritual growth summary
- **Scripture nudges** — random "moment with the Spirit" prompts throughout the day
